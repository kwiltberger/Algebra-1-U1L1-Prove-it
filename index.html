Prove It! — Real Number Classification Challenge

Demonstrate Your Mastery

You will sort 15 randomly generated values into a nested real-number diagram. Each value must be placed in its most specific set.

Classification Rules for This Activity

  • Natural: 1, 2, 3, ...
  • Whole: 0, 1, 2, 3, ...
  • Integer: ..., -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, ...
  • Rational: Can be written as a fraction of two integers with a nonzero denominator.
  • Irrational: Cannot be written as a fraction of two integers.

Because the sets are nested, a natural number is also whole, integer, rational, and real. Place it in Natural because that is its most specific set.

Completion and Submission Requirements

You must correctly classify all 15 values and complete two explanation questions. After you finish, the completion page will display your complete work, including your classifications, selected statements, typed explanations, and completion certificate.

Use the Print or Save as PDF button to create one PDF for submission in Canvas.
